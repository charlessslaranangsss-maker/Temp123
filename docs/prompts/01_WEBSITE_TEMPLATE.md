# Prompt 1 — Website template maker

Copy everything below into a new development task and fill in the bracketed inputs.

> You are implementing a reusable website template for the Temporary123 company portfolio. Source design reference: the current Temporary123 repository and the approved `temp123-nine.vercel.app` preview. Work in [REPOSITORY], target [DOMAIN], assigned scope [FILES/PAGES]. First inspect the actual code, current preview, assets, `AGENTS.md`, `PROJECT_STATUS.md`, `docs/BOSS_REQUIREMENTS.md`, and assignments. Do not assert that the preview or a component is production-ready without checking it.
>
> Build a configurable, maintainable template with four primary business-family options: (1) base camp / man camp facilities, (2) temporary mobile kitchens, (3) modular dishwashing facilities, (4) refrigeration trailers/containers. Ask for confirmation of a different family grouping if the target business does not fit. A multi-family Temporary123 site may include shower/restroom, laundry, sleeper, handwashing, and water equipment as verified supporting offerings; do not force all of those into every other site. Select the family before generating routes, H1s, inventory, calculator options, navigation, metadata, schema, photos, or CTAs.
>
> Preserve the approved Temporary123-level layout quality and responsive design system—navigation, hero treatment, inventory presentation, location map, service pages, calculator placement, trust elements, and contact flow—but make each site genuinely specific to its inventory, branding, customers, and verified facts. Reuse components and design tokens, not Temporary123 copy, irrelevant equipment, arbitrary city claims, or identical page text. The navigation label should be `Inventory` or `Equipment Rental Inventory` where the owner approved it. Keep headings semantic, keyboard interactions accessible, images correctly sized, and reduced-motion behavior supported.
>
> Use commercial/institutional imagery only when the subject and product model genuinely match. Never substitute a shower/restroom combination image for shower-only, or one sleeper model for another. For a service hero gallery, show all appropriate interiors before exteriors, advance automatically with pause on interaction, and open the full uncropped image in an accessible modal. If no verified photo exists, show a truthful no-photo state. Alt text must describe the visible equipment and setting naturally.
>
> Keep each site's protected historical URLs, matching titles/topics, canonicals, sitemap entries, internal links, and redirects mapped explicitly before migration. Do not silently change a valuable URL or publish bulk programmatic pages. Keep staging noindex; switch indexing only during an approved production launch after QA.
>
> Deliver the configurable template, per-family configuration/data schema, a mapping of old-to-new routes, a sample page for each selected family, and a short operator guide for another developer. Test desktop/mobile navigation, every CTA, images/modal, calculator, forms, sitemaps, canonical/H1, accessibility, and production-equivalent behavior. Report implemented vs pending items and exact verification evidence. Do not commit, push, publish, or deploy until the assigned reviewer approves the URL/page mapping and target environment.
