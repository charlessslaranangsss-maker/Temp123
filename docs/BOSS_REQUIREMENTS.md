# Boss Requirements — Temporary123

This file consolidates current instructions so all tasks work from the same interpretation. Add dated clarifications rather than silently replacing earlier requirements.

## Location-page subject matter

- Location pages must describe temporary base-camp and commercial/institutional deployment needs, not generic tourism or residential content.
- Keep the keyword family natural: equipment/service + commercial facility or operational need + rental intent + city/state.
- Describe relevant access and logistics only when accurate, such as waterfront access, major corridors, constrained delivery approaches, staging space, utility needs, and emergency deployment.
- Avoid thin, repetitive, or doorway-style city pages.

## Equipment priority

- Include the new 22-foot shower trailer with three sinks where relevant.
- Treat it as a flagship shower option alongside shower/restroom combination units.
- Keep equipment specifications accurate and do not invent capacities or features.

## Image policy

- Use commercial or institutional environments: prisons, hospitals, nursing homes, man camps, hotels/hospitality locations, military locations, and industrial facilities.
- Do not use homes or small-business backgrounds.
- Prefer varied, contextually appropriate backgrounds across pages and responsive sizes.
- Add concise alt text connected to the actual image and target topic. Do not stuff keywords.

## Valuable URL and backlink preservation

- Export valuable URLs and backlink data from Ahrefs before removals or migrations.
- Preserve the exact URL and slug for valuable pages whenever practical.
- Preserve original topic and search intent while improving the content.
- Every rebuilt valuable URL must return HTTP 200, use the intended self-referencing canonical, be internally linked, and appear in the correct sitemap/section.
- Place city/state pages under locations, equipment/service pages under their matching service section, and informational pages under resources/articles.
- Do not place every valuable URL in the article section.
- Maintain an owner-visible Protected URL/Authority Register with URL, metrics, new title, section, rebuild status, HTTP status, canonical, sitemap, testing, Search Console submission, and indexing evidence.

## Initial validation and controlled release

- Select the top 25 valuable URLs for the first controlled test.
- Verify exact slugs, HTTP 200, indexability, self-referencing canonicals, internal links, content quality, and sitemap inclusion.
- Submit individual priority URLs through Google Search Console URL Inspection when authorized and record the result. Requesting indexing does not guarantee indexing.
- Do not release all planned pages at once.
- After the first controlled test, release only completed, distinct pages in measured batches of roughly 200–1,000 every four days, while monitoring crawl/indexing behavior and quality signals.
- Pause rollout when material canonical, crawl, duplicate-content, indexing, or quality problems appear.

## Central dashboard request

The requested owner-facing dashboard should eventually cover all company websites and include:

- Portfolio migration and health overview
- Explainable SEO health scoring
- Mobile/desktop speed and Core Web Vitals
- Technical SEO issue lists with clickable URLs
- Protected authority URL and backlink register
- Google visibility estimates clearly distinguished from verified Search Console indexing
- Keyword and city-size tracking
- Content-quality checks
- Optional secure Google Search Console connection
- Ahrefs, crawler, sitemap, city/population, and manual imports initially
- Owner controls, exports, assignments, notes, approvals, and change history

The dashboard is a separate product workstream and must receive its own MVP scope and acceptance criteria before broad implementation.

## Nationwide calculator request

- One calculator page per website.
- Inputs: state/city/ZIP, equipment type, trailer length, rental dates, number of people, name, phone, and email.
- CTA: `Get Starting Estimate / Request Quote`.
- Delivery starting prices: 20 ft $995; add $100 per additional foot through 40 ft (25 ft $1,495; 30 ft $1,995; 35 ft $2,495; 40 ft $2,995).
- Keep rental starting-price data in a structured spreadsheet or data file.
- Keep Sleeper/Bunkbed Trailers and Sleeper Modular Containers as separate choices.
- Expose relevant service-area text as readable static HTML below the calculator; do not rely only on dropdown options.
- Use one stable, website-specific H1.
- Treat displayed prices as starting estimates and keep final quote logic clear.

## Security and evidence

- Never place credentials in this repository.
- Do not report a page as indexed solely from a `site:` search.
- Do not report a page, form, calculation, or deployment as complete without direct testing and recorded evidence.
